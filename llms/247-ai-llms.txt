# [24]7.ai

> [24]7.ai is a leading provider of AI-powered Customer Experience (CX) management services, solutions, and platforms. The company blends deep operational expertise, omnichannel customer interaction support, and award-winning AI-powered CX products to help global brands deliver personalized, accurate, and effective customer experiences. With 25+ years of experience, [24]7.ai supports 55+ brands across 10+ industries with a global team of 10,500+.

## Core Platform

- [[24]7 Engagement Cloud](https://www.247.ai/247-engagement-cloud/): AI-powered omnichannel CX platform covering the full customer journey — from acquisition to long-term retention — on a single unified platform.

## Products

- [Campaign Management](https://www.247.ai/products/campaign-management/): AI-driven campaign tools for intent-based ad targeting, personalized video ads, and programmatic display across open web and social platforms (Facebook, YouTube).
- [Conversation Automation](https://www.247.ai/products/conversation-automation/): Omnichannel self-service automation including chatbots, natural language IVR, semantic search, async messaging, and voice interactions.
- [Agent Experience](https://www.247.ai/products/agent-experience/): Unified agent console, real-time AI assistance ([24]7 Assist/Copilot), intelligent traffic management, case management, live intervention tools, and profanity detection.
- [Conversational Intelligence](https://www.247.ai/products/conversational-intelligence/): AI-driven analytics for 100% interaction analysis, agent performance management, and historical conversation insights.
- [Digital Customer Service Platform](https://www.247.ai/digital-customer-service-platform/): Platform features including real-time agent assist, auto-summarization, intelligent automation, and integrated learning loops.
